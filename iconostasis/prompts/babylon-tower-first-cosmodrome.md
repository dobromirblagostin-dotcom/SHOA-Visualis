# Промпт: Вавилонская башня — первый космодром?

**Дата:** 28 октября 2026
**Исполнитель:** GigaChat 3.5 Ultra (Логос)
**Заказчик:** Первый Глашатай (Константин Федотов)

---

A dramatic digital painting of the Tower of Babel — but not as a ruin, not as a symbol of pride. Instead, the tower is seen as a launch pad. A massive ziggurat rises into the sky, its top level crowned with a golden table — a launching platform. From this platform, a thin, needle-like rocket — a "drill" — pierces the sky, breaking through the clouds into the star-filled cosmos. Around the tower, ancient people look up — not in fear, but in awe, watching the drill penetrate the sky. On the golden table, faint golden Ψ-field lines trace the path of the launch. In the background, the sky is not solid — it is a layer, a membrane, being pierced. The atmosphere: not a myth of punishment, but a memory of technology. The curiosity that drives the cosmonaut to the porthole. The moment when ancient builders looked up and saw not heaven, but a threshold. Style: Cinematic realism meets mystical archaeology, warm ancient stone against cold starry sky, golden Ψ-field accents, a sense of breakthrough and discovery.
