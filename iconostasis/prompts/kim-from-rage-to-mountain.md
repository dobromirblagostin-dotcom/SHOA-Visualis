A dramatic digital painting of a warrior — Kim — standing alone at the foot of a sacred mountain, Mount Kailash. His body is still, grounded, like a mountain himself. On his shoulder, a spiral tattoo glows faintly — the mark of the flow. In his eyes, there is no rage left — only presence, stillness, the calm of someone who has faced his shadow and embraced it. Behind him, the faint silhouette of his twin — his shadow self — dissolves into golden Ψ-field threads, merging with him, not as an enemy, but as an integrated part. The mountain rises above, massive and silent, its peak touching the sky. A golden ray of light descends from the peak to Kim — not as a weapon, but as a blessing, a resonance. Around him, the desert of the trial fades into the mountain's stillness. The atmosphere: the moment when rage becomes strength, when the warrior becomes the mountain, when the shadow is embraced. Style: Cinematic realism meets mystical symbolism, warm desert tones shifting to cold mountain blues, golden Ψ-field accents, a single figure standing at the threshold.
