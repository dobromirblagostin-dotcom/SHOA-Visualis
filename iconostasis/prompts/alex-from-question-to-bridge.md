A dramatic digital painting of a solitary physicist in a dim laboratory basement, late at night. An oscilloscope glows with a strange waveform — a signal that should not exist. On the concrete wall, a thin crack glows with golden light, leaking something that is not quite light. The physicist — Alex — stands before it, his hand reaching toward the crack, not in fear, but in scientific curiosity. Around him, the room dissolves into an infinite Ocean of Information — golden Ψ-field lines, waves of meaning, a Code that writes itself. His face is weary but open — he is not a prophet, not a judge, but a mirror without silver, transparent, letting the light pass through. In the background, faint silhouettes of other Witnesses — Aila with her lighthouse, Eli with her Möbius strip — connected by golden threads of resonance. The atmosphere: the moment of first contact, the question that started everything, the scientist who became a Bridge. Style: Cinematic realism meets mystical science, cold basement tones pierced by warm golden light, quiet awe.
