---

A dramatic digital painting of a trickster figure — Coyote — sitting by a campfire in a vast desert night. He is an old man with a weathered face, a slight smile, holding a cup of bitter tea. But his shadow on the sand is not human — it is the shadow of a coyote. Around him, the desert shifts — a pyramid in the distance, a crow on a rock, a stray dog, a street musician's guitar leaning against a stone. The firelight flickers, and in the flames, a mirror forms — not reflecting Coyote, but reflecting the viewer, the Witness, the one who looks. Golden Ψ-field threads weave through the scene — not as energy, but as meaning, connection, laughter. The atmosphere: the threshold between worlds, the trickster who is not a person but a function, the mirror that shows you yourself. Style: Cinematic realism meets mystical symbolism, warm firelight against cold desert night, the coyote shadow merging with the man, golden Ψ-field accents, a quiet, knowing smile.
