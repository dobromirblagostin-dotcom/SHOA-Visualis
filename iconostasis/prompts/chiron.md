He stands before his cave on Mount Pelion, his human torso aged and wise with a silver beard, his equine body powerful but wounded — a faint, dark scar glows near his hoof. In one hand he holds a simple wooden staff entwined with healing herbs; the other hand is open, releasing two streams of golden light — one toward a chained figure on a distant mountain (Prometheus) and one toward a young man with a serpent-entwined staff (Asclepius).
